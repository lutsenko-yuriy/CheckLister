# N/A-80: Localization: German, French and Russian

## Notes

## Debrief summary

### 2026-09-24

**What went well**
- The custom typed `shared/i18n/` module (rejecting `i18next`/`react-i18next`
  in favor of ~50 hand-typed strings and hand-written CLDR plural rules)
  worked out well — the owner was pleasantly surprised the off-the-shelf
  frameworks didn't fit given how small the string count and how heavy the
  `Intl.PluralRules` polyfill requirement on Hermes would have been.

**What was hard or surprising**
- The owner's main concern with a custom-built i18n module (rather than an
  established library) is long-term maintainability: will adding a new
  language, or fixing a translation bug, end up more complex than it would
  be with a framework? Mitigated for now by the module's typed key parity
  and the `locales.test.ts` / `nativeLocales.test.ts` drift tests, which
  turn a missed step into an immediate, loud failure rather than a silent
  gap — documented as an explicit "Adding a new language" checklist in
  `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` so this stays checkable rather than tribal
  knowledge.

**What to change**
- `shared/i18n/` is a generic subdomain (DDD sense) — domain-agnostic and
  reusable beyond this app. The owner wants to consider giving it a harder
  module boundary (its own package/workspace) rather than just an
  organizationally-separate directory under `shared/`. Filed as a follow-up
  backlog ticket rather than scoped now.
