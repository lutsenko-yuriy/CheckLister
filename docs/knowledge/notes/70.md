## Debrief summary

### 2026-09-23

**What went well**
- The theming migration across all three WUs landed cleanly and the app looks good in dark mode.

**What was hard or surprising**
- The ticket picked up two side quests mid-flight: CI build speed (CheL-72) and a splash-screen redesign, both handled inline rather than deferred. The cumulative session length across all of this was exhausting on its own, independent of any single side quest.
- The CI slowness was a genuine blocker (wasted time waiting on release builds to test against), so pausing to fix it was the right call — and it's already resolved.
- The splash screen was a discretionary "I don't like how this looks" call that could have waited until the ticket was done, but got folded into WU3 live instead of being scoped as its own ticket.

**What to change**
- When a new, tangential ask comes up mid-session that isn't part of the current ticket/WU's planned scope, proactively ask whether it belongs in the current ticket or should become its own follow-up ticket, instead of silently implementing it inline. Added to `docs/workflows/FEATURE.md`'s step 3 as a new "User-initiated scope additions" rule alongside the existing self-discovered "Scope-expansion discoveries" rule.
